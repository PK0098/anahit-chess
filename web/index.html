§ 01 / How it works

The rules,
so far.briefly.

The format depends on how many of you show up. The clock doesn't.

Five rules. That's it. Read them once, then go find someone to play.

Need a clock?

Grab the free Chess.com clock app. Set it to 10 + 0.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play
  1. 1.♜
    Format: decided by headcount
    Once registration closes, the number of players picks the format:
    Round robin league
    Smaller field. Everyone plays everyone once. Win 3 · Draw 1 · Loss 0.
    Knockout bracket
    Bigger field. Lose and you're out. Dramatic, but efficient.
  2. 1.♜
    One game each
    Every pair of players plays each other once — a single round robin. Colors are decided by coin toss (or rock-paper-scissors, we're not picky).
  3. 2.♛
    Ten minutes each
    Whatever the format, every game is played with a 10-minute timer per player (10+0, no increment). This one is not up for a vote.
  4. 3.♚
    3 · 1 · 0
    Win = 3 points, Draw = 1 point, Loss = 0 points.
  5. 3.4.♝
    Play nice
    Standard chess rules apply. Touch-move, be nice, have fun.
  6. 4.5.♞
    Your game, your schedule
    Players arrange their own games during the tournament period and report the result.
  7. 5.6. (unofficial)
    Losing to the intern is not a bug.
    It's a feature.
